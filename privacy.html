Privacy Policy — Cassino Royale

Effective date: July 16, 2026 · Cassino Royale (iOS), published by PikeSquare, LLC

The short version

Cassino Royale does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Everything the app saves lives only on your device:

  • Game statistics (games played, wins, streaks)
  • Coin balance, unlocked decks, and achievements
  • Saved games in progress
  • Your settings (theme, deck, sound, and similar preferences)

This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Apple services the app uses

Two optional features are provided directly by Apple, under Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • Game Center — if you choose to play online or view the leaderboard, Apple's Game Center handles sign-in and matchmaking. We receive no personal information from it. The leaderboard shows only your Game Center alias and win count, managed by Apple.
  • In-App Purchase — the optional Premium unlock is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

If you never use these features, the app makes no network connections at all.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, contains no gambling, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes what data the app handles, this policy will be updated first and the change described in the App Store release notes.
